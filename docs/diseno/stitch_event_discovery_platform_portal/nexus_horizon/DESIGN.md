## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an elevated, accessible, tech-forward aesthetic tailored for live event discovery, community management, and ticket tiering. The visual direction balances the crisp utility of a modern SaaS management tool with the ambient excitement of premier community gatherings.

### Personality & Tone
- **Authoritative & Dependable:** Crisp structural lines and deep navy anchors project security and operational precision.
- **Vibrant & Communal:** Kinetic accents in fresh green and primary blue convey activity, registration momentum, and real-time social presence.
- **Pristine Modernism:** Heavy reliance on intentional whitespace, balanced contrast, and disciplined hierarchy ensures dense agendas and ticket matrices remain frictionless to scan.

### Design Movement
Modern Corporate balanced with Scandinavian-inspired structural minimalism. Elevation is conveyed primarily through subtle border delineations, tinted neutral containers, and ambient shadows, allowing rich event imagery and typography to lead the experience.

## Colors

The palette leverages a high-contrast architectural approach:

- **Primary Blue (`#1A73E8`):** The primary engine for user interaction. Reserved for primary calls-to-action (CTAs), live calendar indicators, step progression, and active navigation states.
- **Deep Navy (`#0D1B2A`):** The grounding anchor. Applied to primary headings, high-priority icons, inverted container sections, and brand framing to enforce contrast and structural gravity.
- **Tertiary Fresh Green (`#2BC48A`):** The energetic spark. Used strictly for status merits, verified organizer badges, "Early Bird" pricing flags, confirmed RSVP checkmarks, and positive capacity signals.
- **Neutral Soft Gray (`#E6E9EC`):** The structural boundary. Used for subtle background canvasing, division strokes, inactive card borders, and segmented control containers.
- **Surface Foundations:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) serves as the base card surface, elevated against an ambient slate canvas (`#F8FAFC`), bounded by `#E6E9EC` hairline outlines.

## Typography

The type scale balances geometric personality with functional precision:

- **Display & Headline Levels (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Provide contemporary punch and friendly geometry. Tighter negative letter spacing on larger sizes maintains architectural density without compromising legibility.
- **Body & Technical Levels (Inter):** Deliver hyper-legible execution for agendas, speaker biographies, pricing tables, and transactional checkout forms.
- **Micro Labels (Inter 600/700):** Modest uppercase tracking is permitted strictly for `label-sm` badges (e.g., ticket categories, status tags, time stamps) to ensure instant visual isolation.

## Layout & Spacing

A strictly bounded 8-point spatial model enforces cadence throughout dashboards, event grids, and checkout flows.

### Grid Anatomy
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout, `margin-lg` (3rem/48px) side gutters, 1.5rem/24px column gutters. Maximum content canvas constrained to 1280px for desktop reading ergonomics.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout, `margin` (1.5rem/24px), 1rem/16px column gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `margin-sm` (1rem/16px), 1rem/16px column gutters. Event feeds reflow into single-column vertical stacks.

### Rhythm Application
- **Micro Spacing (`space-xs` to `space-sm`):** Label-to-input pairings, icon-to-text lockups, status pill internals.
- **Component Padding (`space-md` to `space-lg`):** Event card internal padding, dropdown interiors, navigation bar vertical clearance.
- **Macro Layout (`space-xl` and section multiples):** Separation between featured event hero blocks, chronological agenda clusters, and tiered ticket grids.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained via low-contrast borders coupled with cool-toned, ambient shadows that draw depth from the `#0D1B2A` foundation:

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** `#F8FAFC` background. Elements rely strictly on borders (`1px solid #E6E9EC`) for structural partition without drop shadows.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Modules):** Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`), `1px solid #E6E9EC`, shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(13, 27, 42, 0.04), 0 4px 12px rgba(13, 27, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States & Drawers):** `1px solid #D5DAE1`, shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(13, 27, 42, 0.06), 0 12px 24px -4px rgba(13, 27, 42, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Toolbars):** Borderless or faint border (`rgba(13, 27, 42, 0.08)`), shadow: `0 16px 32px -4px rgba(13, 27, 42, 0.12), 0 24px 48px -8px rgba(13, 27, 42, 0.08)`.
- **Inverted Panels:** Occasional dark command zones or VIP tiers utilize a flat `#0D1B2A` surface with subtle borders (`rgba(230, 233, 236, 0.12)`), providing immediate contextual contrast without shadow clutter.

## Shapes

A balanced geometric corner profile (`roundedness: 2`) ensures tactile accessibility without compromising technical rigor:

- **Base Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to standard interactive primitives: text fields, compact buttons, dropdown items, filter chips, and inner badges.
- **Large Radius (1rem / 16px):** Applied to standard event cards, checkout ticket modules, pricing tiers, and modular panels.
- **Extra-Large Radius (1.5rem / 24px):** Reserved for hero feature banners, modal windows, and main container sheets.
- **Circular / Full Rounding:** Restricted strictly to user avatars, quick-filter pill toggles, and floating notification dots.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#1A73E8` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.5rem` border-radius. On hover: darken background by 6% with an ambient blue shadow tint. Focused: `2px` offset ring in `#1A73E8`.
- **Secondary:** Transparent surface with `1.5px solid #0D1B2A`, `#0D1B2A` text. On hover: solid `#0D1B2A` fill with `#FFFFFF` text.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** No background, `#0D1B2A` text with subtle `#E6E9EC` background tint on hover.
- **Destructive:** Deep crimson accenting, avoiding clash with `#2BC48A` status accents.

### Cards
- **Event Discovery Card:** White card base, `1rem` radius, `1px solid #E6E9EC` outline. Features a 16:9 aspect image header with a localized `#2BC48A` category or verified badge placed top-left. Card interior incorporates structured metadata rows (Date, Location, Capacity) followed by a persistent action bar.
- **Tiered Ticket Card:** Elevated white card featuring a distinct navy-bordered accent strip on selection, radio button selector, dynamic pricing breakdown, and seat availability indicators.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills:** Pill radius, padding `0.25rem 0.625rem`. Background set to a 10% tint of `#2BC48A` with solid `#1E8F64` text for active, verified, or published events.
- **Filter Tags:** `0.5rem` radius, neutral gray surface (`#F1F3F5`) with `#0D1B2A` text. When selected: transitions to `#0D1B2A` fill with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Form Inputs
- **Text & Select Fields:** `0.5rem` radius, `1px solid #E6E9EC`, pure white background, 44px minimum touch height, `Inter 15px` typography. Active focus transitions border directly to `#1A73E8` with a 3px soft outer ring (`rgba(26, 115, 232, 0.15)`).

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `0.25rem` radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios. Idle: `1.5px solid #D5DAE1`. Checked: Solid `#1A73E8` fill with crisp white vector marks.

### Lists & Agendas
- **Agenda Timeline Item:** Left-aligned chronological rail. Time stamps set in `Plus Jakarta Sans` semi-bold (`#0D1B2A`), vertical hairline separator (`#E6E9EC`), expanding accordion module with speaker thumbnails (`0.5rem` radius) and room assignment labels.
